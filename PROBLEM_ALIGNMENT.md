# Problem Statement Alignment

Evaluation criterion: Problem Statement Alignment — complete end-to-end delivery of Challenge 6 requirements R1–R26.

## 1. Challenge 6 Goal & Core Capabilities

> _"Create a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. The application should suggest low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices."_

- **Zero-Key Full-Stack Execution**: Works immediately without API keys via live Open-Meteo APIs (`src/services/open-meteo.ts`), offline Demo Mode presets (`src/services/demo-data.ts`), and deterministic grounded fallback intelligence (`src/services/assistant-responses.ts`), switching cleanly to Gemini/Anthropic when keys exist (`src/services/assistant.ts`).
- **Complete Urban Smog Workflow**: Dual US/European AQI + winter inversion detection, 6-pollutant WHO 2021 comparison, accessible 48h/7d forecast charts + data tables, 7-persona family & lifestyle guidance (commute, cooking, filtration) with indoor air hacks, interactive routine/impact calculator (`PM2.5` µg avoided, `CO2e`/`NOx` saved), $O(n)$ activity planner, 3-city comparison, `localStorage` threshold alerts, and trilingual UI (`en`/`hi`/`ta`).
