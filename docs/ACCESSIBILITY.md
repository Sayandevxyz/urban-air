# Accessibility Conformance & Design — Urban Air Quality Assistant

## 1. Conformance Target & Automated Verification

- **Target Standard**: **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** (with AAA touch targets, contrast ratios, and plain-language health literacy goals where practical).
- **Verified Outcomes**:
  - **Playwright `@axe-core/playwright` (`tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts`)**: **0 violations** across `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa` in both **Light mode** and **Dark mode** on Desktop (`1280×720`) and Mobile (`Pixel 7`).
  - **Component `jest-axe` (`tests/components/*.test.tsx`)**: **0 violations** across Search, Dashboard, Forecast Chart/Table, Persona Guidance, Indoor Hacks, Routines & Impact, Activity Planner, Comparison Table, Local Alerts Form, and AI Assistant.
  - **Google Lighthouse Accessibility**: **100 / 100** (Desktop and Mobile).
  - **320 px Responsive Check (`tests/e2e/urban-air.spec.ts`)**: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= document.documentElement.clientWidth` verified at `320×640` across desktop and mobile profiles with active tables and chat history.

## 3. Chart & Visualization Alternative Policy

Every chart surface provides exact numeric parity in standard semantic markup:

- **48-Hour Hourly Forecast**: Toggle between interactive SVG polyline (`HourlyChart.tsx`, `role="img"` with min/max `aria-label`) and a scrollable semantic HTML `<table>` listing UTC timestamps, US AQI, EAQI, PM2.5, NO2, and O3 (`ForecastDataTable.tsx`, `ForecastSection.tsx`).
- **7-Day Daily Trend**: Toggle between stripe-patterned SVG column bars (`DailyTrend.tsx`) and a semantic HTML `<table>` listing Date, Avg US AQI, Min–Max US AQI, and Avg PM2.5 (`ForecastDataTable.tsx`).
