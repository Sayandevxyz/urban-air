# Requirements Specification — Urban Air Quality Assistant (Challenge 6)

> **Source of Truth**:
>
> 1. Official Challenge 6 Problem Statement: _"Create a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. The application should suggest low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices."_
> 2. Technical, Security, Efficiency, Testing, and Accessibility Specifications.

## Documented Assumptions & Literal Interpretations

1. **Dual AQI Scales**: Both US AQI (0–500 scale) and European AQI (EAQI 0–100+ scale) display simultaneously; upstream values are validated and supplemented by deterministic US EPA breakpoint calculation in `src/domain/aqi-breakpoints.ts` so live and offline Demo Mode remain 100% consistent.
2. **Winter Smog & Inversion Detection**: "Severe winter smog" is evaluated deterministically from high particulate burden (PM2.5 >= 55.5 µg/m³ or PM10 >= 155 µg/m³ or US AQI >= 151) and stagnant/trapped urban pollution signatures (`src/domain/aqi.ts`), surfacing targeted advisories anytime for reproducibility.
3. **Zero-Key Guarantee**: Runs end-to-end with zero paid API keys via Open-Meteo (`src/services/open-meteo.ts`), seeded offline Demo presets (`src/services/demo-data.ts`), and deterministic grounded fallback answers (`src/services/assistant-responses.ts`) when `GEMINI_API_KEY` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` are absent.
4. **Lifestyle Tailoring**: Resident inputs cover commute mode (`car`, `transit`, `two_wheeler`, `walk_cycle`), household cooking/heating energy (`gas_biomass`, `electric_induction`), and indoor filtration (`none`, `hepa`, `diy_box_fan`).
5. **Direct Impact Methodology**: Personal PM2.5 inhalation avoidance (µg) and community CO2e / NOx savings (g) use transparent EPA/DEFRA-aligned factors documented in `src/domain/impact.ts`.
