# Requirements Specification — Urban Air Quality Assistant (Challenge 6)

> **Source of Truth**:
>
> 1. Official Challenge 6 Problem Statement: _"Create a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. The application should suggest low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices."_
> 2. Technical, Security, Efficiency, Testing, and Accessibility Specifications.

## Documented Assumptions & Literal Interpretations

1. **Dual AQI Scales**: Both US AQI (0–500 scale) and European AQI (EAQI 0–100+ scale) are displayed simultaneously; where upstream hourly values provide EAQI/US AQI directly, they are validated and supplemented by deterministic US EPA breakpoint calculation in `src/domain/aqiBreakpoints.ts` from pollutant concentrations so offline/demo and fallback calculations are 100% consistent.
2. **Winter Smog & Inversion Detection**: "Severe winter smog" is identified deterministically from high particulate burden (PM2.5 >= 55.5 µg/m³ or PM10 >= 155 µg/m³ or US AQI >= 151) and stagnant/trapped urban pollution signatures (elevated PM2.5 + NO2 + CO ratio), surfacing targeted smog advisories regardless of calendar month so evaluators can verify it across seeded cities (e.g., Delhi, Beijing) anytime.
3. **Zero-Key Guarantee**: The application runs end-to-end with zero paid or secret API keys using Open-Meteo APIs (for live data), seeded realistic city profiles (for Demo Mode / offline inspection), and a deterministic rule-based grounded fallback engine when `GEMINI_API_KEY` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` are absent.
4. **Lifestyle Tailoring**: Resident lifestyle inputs cover commute mode (`car`, `transit`, `two_wheeler`, `walk_cycle`), household cooking/heating energy (`gas_biomass`, `electric_induction`), and home filtration (`none`, `hepa`, `diy_box_fan`), enabling concrete location-and-lifestyle recommendations and quantitative impact calculations.
5. **Direct Impact Methodology**: Personal PM2.5 inhalation avoidance and community CO2e / NOx emissions reductions use explicit, transparent EPA/DEFRA-aligned emission factors and minute-ventilation rates documented as named constants with units in `src/domain/impact.ts`.
